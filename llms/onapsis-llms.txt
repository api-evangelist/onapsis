# Onapsis
> Onapsis is the pioneer in cybersecurity and compliance solutions for cloud, hybrid, and on-premise business-critical applications. As the proven market leader and an SAP-endorsed partner, the Onapsis Platform delivers vulnerability management, threat detection and response, change assurance, and continuous compliance for applications from vendors like SAP, Oracle, and Salesforce.
